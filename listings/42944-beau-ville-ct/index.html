Property Details

Completely Renovated. Resort-Style Backyard.

Welcome to this beautifully renovated four bedroom home offering approximately 2,056 square feet of modern living on a quiet cul de sac in the heart of Lancaster. Completely updated from top to bottom, this turnkey property delivers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and outdoor enjoyment.

Step inside to a bright, open layout featuring waterproof vinyl flooring throughout and a fully remodeled kitchen that flows seamlessly into the eat in dining area and living room, creating an inviting space for everyday living and entertaining. Four spacious bedrooms and fully renovated bathrooms have been thoughtfully updated with modern finishes and a clean, contemporary design. The generously sized primary suite is a true retreat, offering plenty of room to relax along with a beautifully finished standup shower in the en suite bath. One of the secondary bedrooms is graced with its own stone fireplace, a rare and elegant touch that transforms the room into a cozy sanctuary, perfect as a guest suite, reading retreat, or private den. An indoor laundry room and a three car garage add everyday convenience.

Major system upgrades include updated electrical, updated plumbing, central HVAC, a brand new water heater, and fully paid off solar panels, providing long term energy savings and peace of mind for the next owner.

Step outside to your private backyard retreat, complete with a sparkling inground pool and spa and a covered patio that is perfect for relaxing, entertaining, and enjoying Southern California living year round.

With extensive renovations, energy efficient paid off solar, and a resort style backyard, this move in ready home is waiting for its next owner. A rare opportunity to own a fully renovated four bedroom pool home with paid off solar in Lancaster. Schedule your showing today.
